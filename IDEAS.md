# Ideas

Bigger directions for this project, in plain language. Written 2026-08-19.
Ordered by group, not priority. Implementation-level next steps live in `PLAN.md`.

## Getting content in

1. **Trickle, don't burst.** Fetch a few videos every so often all day instead of 40
   at once. The limit is about how fast you ask, not how much you want.
2. **Resume on its own.** It already knows which videos it missed. It should not wait
   for you to ask again.
3. **Learn how long blocks really last** instead of guessing. Time them, use the real
   number.

## Beyond YouTube

4. **Add podcasts, newsletters, articles.** The archive does not care where the words
   came from.
5. **Follow people, not channels** — the same person across YouTube, their podcast, and
   their writing, in one place.

## Asking it questions

6. **Ask a question, get an answer with quotes and timestamps.** Half-built today.
7. **"How did their view on this change?"** — line up what someone said over months,
   in order.
8. **Show how topics connect** — which tools, people, and ideas keep showing up
   together.
9. **A short summary per video**, so you can skim a hundred without reading one.

## Trusting it

10. **Always separate what was actually said from what the AI concluded about it.**
11. **Flag stale takes.** A six-month-old opinion about AI tools is often just wrong now.

## Using it daily

12. **A weekly briefing** — what the people you follow said this week, in five bullets.
13. **Leave it running** on a small always-on machine so it collects while you sleep.
14. **Make it your assistant's memory by default**, not a tool you have to remember to
    reach for.

## One practical limit

15. **Built for hundreds, not tens of thousands.** Search currently reads the whole
    archive every time. Fine now, slow later.

## If only two

**1 and 12.** The first makes the archive fill itself without babysitting. The second is
the thing you would actually open every week, which is what turns this from a database
into a habit.
